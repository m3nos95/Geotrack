Standard Specs defer density; Table B-1 requires T89/T90 at rate

Earthwork acceptance in Standard Specs often says only 'compact to X% of maximum density' and points to the Materials Manual, while Table B-1 assigns liquid/plastic limits (T89/T90) at per-yd³ frequencies. That split is easy to miss in project staffing and is ambiguous about whether every density sample also needs Atterbergs.
